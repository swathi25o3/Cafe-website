Our story

We built Rainforest to feel like a cabin you happen to find at the end of a trail.

Our tables are reclaimed wood, our lamps are warm and low, and the shelves are full of books you are welcome to read. Nothing here is rushed.

We brew small-batch coffee, steep loose-leaf herbal teas and bake every morning. Come for a quiet hour, a catch-up with friends, or a rainy afternoon with a book and a blanket.
